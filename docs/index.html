CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Velocity, Elegance & Privacy

All essential tools you need to master your personal finances without clutter, pop-ups, or subscriptions.

⚡

Offline-First & Instant

Powered by local Drift SQLite. Log expenses with zero network latency, zero buffering, and full offline autonomy.

🛡️

Biometric & Keystore Lock

Universal Fingerprint & Face Recognition via local_auth. Hardened with salted PBKDF2 HMAC-SHA256 and Android Keystore encryption.

🎨

Dual Theme Engine

Seamlessly toggle between serene Botanical Forest (Night Pine & Sage) and high-contrast Pure Cyberpunk (Electric Cyan & Neon Magenta).

🚀

Silky 120 FPS Fluidity

Engineered with RepaintBoundary canvas caching, lazy slivers, and zero shader overdraw for butter-smooth navigation.

📝

5-Second Logging & Calc

Rapid cashflow input with dynamic category badges, mini calculator arithmetic, transfer admin fee tracking, and receipt photos.

📊

Smart Budgets & Daily Pulse

50/30/20 budget pools with adaptive progress bars, rollover balances, and daily financial pulse alerts before overspending.

🤝

Debts, Split Bills & Alerts

Track who owes whom, record partial settlements, split shared dinner bills in 1 tap, and configure due date reminders.

🎯

Savings Goals & Reports

Create milestone funds for emergency stashes or travel. Export clean PDF statements, CSV spreadsheets, or JSON backups.
