Core Features

Engineered for Velocity, Elegance & Privacy

All the essential tools you need to master your personal finances without clutter or compromises.

⚡

Offline-First & Instantaneous

Powered by local Drift SQLite. Launch the app and log expenses instantly with zero network latency, buffering, or waiting.

📊

Smart Category Budgets

Set monthly spending caps per expense category. Monitor your remaining allowance with visual progress indicators before overspending.

🎯

Dedicated Savings Goals

Create dedicated funds for emergency stashes, gadgets, or travel. Make gradual deposits and watch your goals come to life.

📈

Visual Insights & Analytics

Understand where every penny goes with insightful interactive pie diagrams and fl_chart spending trends.

Listens to banking and e-wallet push notifications in background. Transactions wait in a review inbox so you can confirm or edit before saving.
